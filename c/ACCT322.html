عن المقرر

يعرّف بمبادئ المحاسبة الإدارية لدعم قرارات المنظمة: سلوك التكاليف، ونظم التكاليف، وتحليل التكلفة والحجم والربح، والموازنات، والتكاليف المعيارية وتحليل الانحرافات، وتقييم أداء القطاعات.

This course introduces students to the principles and practices of managerial accounting to support organizational decision-making. It covers key concepts such as cost behavior, costing systems, and cost–volume–profit analysis, as well as planning and control tools including budgets, standard costs, and variance analysis. Students will learn to interpret financial information, apply costing methods, evaluate segment performance, and analyze investment and financial decisions using capital budgeting and financial statements. The course emphasizes practical skills, analytical thinking, and professional values, including effective communication and teamwork, preparing students to make informed managerial decisions and present solutions confidently.
