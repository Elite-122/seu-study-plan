عن المقرر

مدخل لنظم المعلومات في الأعمال والإدارة: الأسس التنظيمية والإدارية والتقنية لنظم المعلومات، ودورها في تحسين العمليات ودعم القرار في المنظمة، وطريقة بناء هذه النظم وإدارتها.

This course provides an introduction to information systems for business and management. It is designed to familiarize students with organizational and managerial foundations of systems, the technical foundation for understanding information systems, the role of information systems in enhancing business processes and management decision making across the enterprise, and the process of building and managing systems in organizations. The course will provide, also, students with information systems knowledge that is essential for creating successful and competitive firms. Learning activities include hands-on projects, application software exercises, and a running case study. Face to Face (F2F) and Virtual (online) classes will each be held once per week. Please refer to your class schedule for the days and times of these classes. Your professor will provide instructions on how to attend the virtual class. Participation in these classes is mandatory, and to do well in this course, it is expected that you attend all sessions. If there is some reason you cannot attend a class, please notify your professor.
