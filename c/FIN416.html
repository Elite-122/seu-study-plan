عن المقرر

مدخل شامل للتمويل الإسلامي: خلفية نموه وأهميته، وتحريم الربا كأساس لفهم المصرفية الإسلامية، وأهم العقود المالية الإسلامية وتطبيقاتها.

This course provides a wide-ranging introduction to the subject of Islamic finance, beginning with an introduction which provides a background to the growth in recent years and the reasons why this is important. The prohibition of Riba (interest) is examined in some details as this is fundamental to understand the subject of Islamic Banking and Finance. The course focuses mainly upon the main Islamic financial contract such as Murabaha, Musharaka, Mudharab, Ijara, Altakaful… in order to give a clear image about the difference between the Islamic finance and Interest-based finance.
