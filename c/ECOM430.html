عن المقرر

تدريب عملي لمدة 280 ساعة (عشرة أسابيع عمل على الأقل) في مجال التخصص في جهة حكومية أو خاصة، ويثبت الطالب في نهايته فهمه للممارسات المهنية في تخصصه.

Practical training as a course makes students from the college of Administration and Finance responsible for having to spend a 280 hour of training (equivalent to minimum ten working weeks) in their fields of specialization either in private or public organization. At the completion of training, student will have to show their grasp of most relevant and state of the professional practices in their relevant specialization.
