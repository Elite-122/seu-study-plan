عن المقرر

مقرر يعرّف الطالب بمفاهيم الذكاء الاصطناعي وأساليبه وتطبيقاته في الواقع بطريقة مبسطة، بدون حاجة لرياضيات متقدمة أو برمجة. يستكشف كيف تتخذ أنظمة الذكاء الاصطناعي القرارات وتحل المشكلات، مع أمثلة ودراسات حالة وتطبيق عملي.

This multidisciplinary course introduces university students to the foundational concepts, techniques, and real-world applications of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Designed for learners from all academic backgrounds, the course presents AI in a clear, accessible manner—without requiring advanced mathematics or programming skills. Through engaging examples and practical case studies, students will explore how AI systems make decisions, solve problems, and adapt to various environments. The course also includes hands-on experience with modern AI tools and cloud-based platforms, enabling students to apply AI concepts in realistic scenarios. Ethical and societal implications of AI are also discussed to foster responsible and informed use of intelligent technologies. This course aims to provide students with foundational knowledge and practical skills in the field of Artificial Intelligence (AI), including: - Understanding the basic structure and function of computer systems and their role in enabling AI technologies - Exploring key AI concepts such as intelligent agents, decision-making, and learning from data - Gaining familiarity with major AI methodologies and their applications across various domains
